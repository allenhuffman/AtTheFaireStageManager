AtTheFaire Stage Manager 2026

Modern take on the 2010 scheduling grid. The v0.11 conflict rules, plus a cap on how many acts of a type run at once, performer groups, and per-act exclusivity. Days, stages, categories, groups and acts are configurable, and everything saves in this browser. Nothing here is pushed anywhere.

Pick an act, then click an empty slot to place a show. Click a placed show to remove it.

    Add a category

    A category's colour is the colour its acts show as on the grid — change it here and every act in the category changes. A category with acts in it can't be deleted; move its acts to another category first.

    Max at once is the 2010 conflicts number: at most that many acts of this category at the same time (1 means no two ever overlap).

    Acts in this group

    Acts in the same group share people (or a prop, or a crew) — they can never be on at the same time, on any stage.

    Acts

    ActCategoryShows/dayDurationSetupTeardownBreakCan't run against

    Add an act

    Setup, teardown and break are the buffers the conflict checker enforces: setup before a show, teardown after it, and break between an act's own shows. Deleting an act removes its placed shows.

    "Can't run against" is per-act exclusivity — "Any act" means nothing runs against it, and Except lets one category through (for example, an exclusive joust that still allows childrens acts). It works both ways: an exclusive act already placed blocks anything new against it too.

    Batch edit acts

    Applies to every act, or only the acts in one category. Clicking Apply shows what will change — click it again to confirm.

    Data lives only in this browser (localStorage). No account, no server, nothing is sent anywhere.
